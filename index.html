Before You Begin

Why This Exists

Some moments are too important to simply disappear into the past. So this little website was created as a place to keep them.

There are memories that don't need a special reason to stay with us.

A conversation. A smile. A song. A random joke. A difficult day. A simple moment that somehow became important.

This website brings those moments together — not as a perfect story, but as a collection of real moments that once mattered.

— Taher ❤️
